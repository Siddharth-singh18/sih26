# AyuSync Health Knowledge Source Registry

## Approved Public Health Authorities
Every document ingested into the AyuSync RAG engine is approved by the authoritative source registry:

| Source ID | Agency Name | Apex Level | Allowed For Clinical Guidance | Official Web Domain |
|---|---|---|:---:|---|
| **`WHO`** | World Health Organization | Global Public Health | YES | https://www.who.int |
| **`MOHFW`** | Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India | National Government | YES | https://main.mohfw.gov.in |
| **`ICMR`** | Indian Council of Medical Research | National Research | YES | https://www.icmr.gov.in |
| **`NHA`** | National Health Authority (ABDM / PM-JAY) | National Government | YES | https://nha.gov.in |
| **`NCDC`** | National Centre for Disease Control | National Disease Surveillance | YES | https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in |

## Prohibited Content
- Third-party commercial health portals
- Unverified blogs and community forums
- Unreviewed clinical preprint servers
- Generic AI-generated synthetic articles
